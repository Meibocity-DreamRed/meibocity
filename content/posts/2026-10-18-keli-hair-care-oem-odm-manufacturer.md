1. **Full ingredient transparency.** You should receive a complete ingredient list and, on request, an MSDS for the formula. Keli provides MSDS documentation and can prepare COA (Certificate of Analysis) per batch.

If your target market has specific labelling or restricted-ingredient rules, the factory's R&D team adjusts the formula before sampling — not after you have received a customs penalty.

## Question 3: What Is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

MOQ anxiety is real, especially for first-time importers and e-commerce brands starting small. The answer should never be one rigid number shouted at you over email — it should be a conversation shaped by the product.

Keli's approach to MOQ is flexible and product-based:

- **Ready-stock formulas** (their existing shampoo and hair mask lines, already developed and tested) can be private-labelled with **low MOQs** — ideal for market testing and small first orders.
- **Custom formulations** — a new recipe developed for your brand, your scent, your active ingredients — naturally require a higher MOQ, because the R&D cost and raw-material minimums are real.
- **Volume breaks** reward committed buyers: larger container orders bring better unit pricing, and the factory can hold raw materials against your forecast.

If your volumes are small today, the honest advice is to start with a ready-stock formula at a modest quantity, prove the product in your market, then scale into a custom formula once you have reorder data. A factory that pushes you straight into a huge minimum before you have sold a single bottle is a factory thinking about its own warehouse, not your success.

## Question 4: Can I Approve Samples Before I Order?

This should not even be a question — but it is the one most worth confirming in writing. **Yes: samples come first, production only after your approval.**

The correct process, and the one Keli follows, looks like this:

1. **Brief** — you send your product concept, target price band, packaging idea and destination market.
2. **Sample development** — the R&D team prepares a formula sample (and, if useful, a packaging mock-up).
3. **Your review** — you test texture, scent, lather, packaging and labelling against your expectations.
4. **Formula lock** — after your approval, the master formula is frozen and production runs against that exact specification.
5. **Bulk production with QC gates** — the 5-point inspection checks quantity, colour, scent, packaging integrity and labelling accuracy before shipment.

Serious manufacturers treat the sample as a binding contract with the customer: **what you approve is exactly what you receive.**
