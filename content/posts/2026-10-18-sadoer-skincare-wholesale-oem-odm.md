## Question 2: Is the Formula Safe for My Market?

Formula safety is where brands are born and destroyed. A compliance failure in your destination market does not just cost money — it can get your entire inventory seized. Here is what a responsible supplier should be able to provide, and the questions you should send to SADOER before sampling:

1. **Full ingredient transparency.** Every SADOER product lists its key actives clearly — think hyaluronic acid, vitamin C, niacinamide, collagen, snail secretion filtrate and kojic acid across their serums, masks and treatment lines. Insist on a complete ingredient list for every SKU you plan to import.
2. **Documentation on request.** Ask for the **MSDS (Material Safety Data Sheet)** and a **COA (Certificate of Analysis) per batch**. Any brand that ships internationally — especially to EU, US, Gulf or LATAM markets — should be able to produce these documents as a routine part of sampling.
3. **Destination-market labelling support.** When you move from quote to order, confirm who handles destination-market labelling requirements and restricted-ingredient checks. Get this answer in writing before production, not after customs holds your shipment.

![SADOER salon skincare](assets/uploads/sadoer-category-salon.jpg)

**The takeaway:** never accept "don't worry, the formula is fine" as an answer. Send the ingredient and documentation questions to SADOER **before** you approve a sample — the response time and the quality of the paperwork tells you more than any marketing page.

## Question 4: Can I Approve Samples Before I Order?

Samples are the contract. SADOER's published collaboration process is exactly the workflow you want to see:

1. **Contact** — tell the brand your purchasing needs (which products, which markets, which quantities).
2. **Get a quote and samples** — the brand responds with pricing and sends samples for your review.
3. **Confirm your order** — you approve the samples, the specs and the commercial terms.
4. **Production and shipping** — the order is produced and shipped against the confirmed terms.
