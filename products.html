Our Offering

Product Categories

Six core verticals spanning the entire beauty industry. From professional salon products to retail-ready consumer goods.

01

Hair & Styling

Professional hair care and styling products from China's top manufacturers. Shampoos, conditioners, treatments, coloring systems, perming solutions, hair dryers, styling irons, salon tools, wigs, hair extensions, and accessories. Full OEM/ODM capabilities with custom formulations available.

02

Skincare & Beauty

Comprehensive skincare solutions spanning facial cleansers, toners, serums, moisturizers, masks, eye care, sun protection, body lotions, and anti-aging treatments. Our suppliers specialize in both natural ingredient formulations and advanced dermatological technologies.

03

Cosmetics & Makeup

Complete makeup ranges: foundations, concealers, powders, blushes, eyeshadows, eyeliners, mascaras, lipsticks, lip glosses, and makeup tools. Private label and OEM manufacturing for beauty brands of all sizes. Trend-driven product development with rapid turnaround.

04

Fragrance

Eau de parfum, eau de toilette, cologne, perfume oils, solid perfumes, home fragrance, diffusers, scented candles, and car air fresheners. Custom scent development with experienced perfumers, bottle design, and packaging solutions included.

05

Nail & Lashes

Professional nail art supplies including gel polishes, UV/LED gels, acrylic powders, nail tips, stamping kits, files, buffers, electric nail drills, and UV lamps. Eyelash extensions, lash adhesives, lash lift kits, and brow tinting products for salon and retail channels.

06

Machines & Consumables

Advanced beauty equipment: IPL lasers, RF machines, cavitation devices, hydrafacial machines, oxygen therapy systems, microdermabrasion, LED therapy panels, salon beds, trolleys, magnifying lamps, disposable consumables, and professional packaging solutions.
